FAQ

Questions people usually ask

How long does it take?

Most simple business websites are ready within a few days of us receiving your details. Larger sites with extra pages take longer.

Do I own the website?

Yes. You get the finished files and the published site. There's no lock-in and no monthly fee unless you choose ongoing maintenance.

Where will my website be hosted?

On free static hosting such as GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Cloudflare Pages. These are reliable and cost nothing for a site like this. If you want a custom domain like www.yourbusiness.com, we can set that up as an add-on.

Can I update it myself afterwards?

Yes. Because you get the same files our builder produces, you can open the PageBuilder editor any time, change your details, and download an updated version.

What if I don't have a logo or photos?

No problem. We'll use your business initials in a coloured block and design the layout so it still looks professional without photography.

Can you take online payments?

Not yet. This first version focuses on getting you found and contacted — a WhatsApp button, phone, email, and map link. Online payments are on our roadmap.

Rather do it yourself?

Our builder is free to use. You can always start there and ask us for help later.
